# DM Campaign Module: The Scorpion's Nest

A sample ready-to-run Tabletop adventure module designed for **DFUM: Dungeon Master**.

---

## 📖 Adventure Hook
*The local tavern keeper in Daggerfall reports strange rattling noises from the wine cellar and an abandoned residence down the road. Dangerous vermin have been terrorizing nighttime patrons.*

- **Recommended Party:** 2–4 Adventurers (Levels 1–3).
- **Faction:** Fighters Guild (Faction ID: `41`).
- **Quest Script:** `M0C00Y11` (*Hunt the Giant Scorpion*).

---

## 🗺️ DM Preparation Steps

1. **Setting the Atmosphere:**
   - Open DM Tool (**`Ctrl + F11`**).
   - Set world time to **Dusk (18:00)** or **Midnight (00:00)**.
   - Set weather to **Rain** or **Fog** for immersive mood.

2. **Proposing the Quest:**
   - Open Quest Creator (**`Ctrl + F7`**).
   - In Section 2, select **Fighters Guild (41)** and quest `M0C00Y11`.
   - Click **Offer to Party (Vote)**.
   - Players receive the 60-second parchment prompt and vote **Yes**.

3. **Leading the Party:**
   - In Section 4 of **`Ctrl + F7`**, click **`[View]`** next to the active quest to inspect the target house or dungeon name and coordinates.
   - Walk with the party to the house, or click **`TP to Objective`** (with party warp) to fast travel.

4. **Running the Boss Encounter:**
   - Once inside the target building, the Giant Scorpion spawns at the interior marker.
   - Use FreeCam (**`Ctrl + F8`**) to fly up to the rafters and observe your party's battle tactics.
   - If the party struggles, use **`Ctrl + F11`** to dynamically spawn small vermin waves (`Rat`, `GiantBat`) or adjust lighting!

5. **Turning in the Bounty:**
   - Once slain, the quest counter updates across all party members.
   - Lead the party back to the tavern/guildmaster, click NPC turn-in, and collect your gold and guild reputation!
